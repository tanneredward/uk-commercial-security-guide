Manned Guarding Services

Security officers can support everyday site operations through entry checks, visitor management, patrols and incident reporting. When planning Manned Guarding Services, define the areas to cover, operating hours and escalation procedures so officers have clear responsibilities.

Mobile Security Patrols

Mobile patrols provide visits to premises where continuous on-site staffing may not be required. A patrol plan should identify checkpoints, vulnerable entrances and any signs of damage or unauthorised access that need reporting. Visit frequency should reflect the property's risks and operating hours.

Key Holding and Alarm Response

A clear key holding arrangement helps organisations manage access when an alarm activates outside normal working hours. Keep authorised contacts, access instructions and escalation procedures up to date, and agree how incidents and attendance will be recorded.

CCTV and Remote Monitoring

CCTV can help teams assess incidents and review activity. Plan camera coverage around entrances, perimeter areas and other relevant locations, then check lighting and image quality. Define who reviews alerts, how concerns are escalated and how footage is handled.

Access Control Systems

Access control helps manage who can enter a building or particular areas within it. Review permissions when staff change roles or leave, set expiry dates for temporary access and keep a clear process for lost cards or credentials.

Vacant Property Inspections

Empty premises benefit from documented checks for damaged doors, broken windows, leaks and signs of unauthorised entry. Record findings consistently and assign responsibility for follow-up repairs or further investigation.

Planning Security for Your Site

Start with the property's layout, occupancy and existing arrangements. Identify the main risks, assign responsibilities and review how people, procedures and security systems work together. Revisit the plan when the site's use or operating hours change.
